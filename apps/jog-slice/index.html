Cue outputs & trigger device
tier 1 — host-scheduled no trigger device
Tier 1 fires cues from this computer: roughly ±20 ms and not repeatable between passes — fine for a cue light, not for anything the audience will see A/B'd in a composite. Tier 2 uploads the whole cue list to the device, which runs it off its own clock from a single GO. Reference firmware: firmware/graffik-trig/.
